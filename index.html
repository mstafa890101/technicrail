ABOUT THE ORGANISATION

ENGINEERED FOR ROLLING STOCK RELIABILITY

TechnicRail operates at the intersection of traditional precision engineering and modern rolling stock logistics. Headquartered strategically in Poland with rapid transit links across Central and Western Europe, we minimize fleet downtime for railway operators, leasing companies, and maintenance depots.

01 / QUALITY STANDARDS
TSI & EN COMPLIANCE

All overhauled assemblies and newly fabricated components are produced in strict accordance with European railway standards (EN 15085, EN 10204 3.1).

02 / LOGISTICS HUB
CENTRAL EUROPE POSITION

Located in Poland's premier industrial and railway corridor, enabling fast, road and rail-freight dispatch to Germany, Czech Republic, Austria, and Scandinavia.

03 / SPECIALIZED TOOLING
PRECISION DIAGNOSTICS

Calibrated test benches for pneumatic brake distributors, hydrostatic dampers, ultrasonic non-destructive testing (NDT), and optical dimension verification.

04 / RAPID SOURCING
ACTIVE OEM CHANNELS

Direct supplier agreements with leading European rolling stock manufacturers and OEM component producers for verified origin and warranties.
